npx caddy-subdomain add

Promote your verified subdomain to reverse proxy your Node.js, Python, or Docker app. Auto-detects config and auto-reloads with zero downtime.

Simple NPX Command: auto-detects /etc/caddy/caddy.json & auto-reloads
npx caddy-subdomain add call.keshavsoft.com 5000
Instant Terminal Response
Subdomain: call.keshavsoft.com
Target: localhost:5000
Caddy Reload: Success
Status: Active

Zero-Downtime Hot Promotion

Once your subdomain is verified using probe, your TLS certificates are already issued. Running add call.keshavsoft.com 5000 seamlessly replaces the probe with a reverse proxy route forwarding to localhost:5000.

Because Caddy reloads in memory, existing TLS handshakes are preserved without dropping a single active client connection.
